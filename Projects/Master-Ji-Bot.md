# Master Ji Bot — AI-Powered Career Guidance Chatbot

## 1. Project Overview

Master Ji Bot is an AI-powered conversational chatbot designed to provide career-related guidance to college students. The main idea behind the project was to create a student-friendly virtual assistant that could interact with users through natural-language conversations and help them explore different career opportunities.

The chatbot was designed around the persona of a college student, making its communication style more approachable and relatable to its intended audience. It aimed to help students understand career paths, identify relevant skills, and explore possible directions for their professional development.

This project was developed as a group project with one other team member during the Applied AI training.

## 2. Problem Statement

College students often face difficulties when deciding which career path to choose, understanding the skills required for a particular field, and identifying the steps needed to prepare for future opportunities.

Career-related information is available through many online platforms, but students may find it difficult to identify information that is relevant to their interests and current level of knowledge.

Master Ji Bot was planned as an AI-powered assistant to make career guidance more accessible through simple, interactive, and student-friendly conversations.

## 3. Project Objectives

The main objectives of Master Ji Bot were:

- To develop an AI-powered chatbot focused on college students.
- To provide career-related guidance through natural-language conversations.
- To help students explore different career paths and professional opportunities.
- To provide information about relevant technical and professional skills.
- To make career-related discussions more interactive and understandable.
- To explore the practical use of LLMs and prompt engineering in chatbot development.

## 4. Target Users

The primary target users of Master Ji Bot were college students, particularly those who are exploring career options or trying to understand the skills required for different professional fields.

The chatbot was intended to support students who may have questions about career selection, technical skills, learning paths, internships, and future opportunities.

## 5. Key Features and Functional Areas

The project was planned around the following functional areas:

**Conversational Interaction:** Allowing students to interact with the chatbot using natural-language questions.

**Career Guidance:** Helping users explore different career options and understand possible professional directions.

**Skill-Related Guidance:** Providing information about technical and professional skills associated with different career fields.

**Student-Friendly Communication:** Maintaining an approachable communication style suitable for college students.

**AI-Generated Responses:** Using LLM capabilities to generate responses based on user queries and the chatbot's instructions.

## 6. AI Concepts Used

### Large Language Models (LLMs)

LLMs were explored for their ability to understand natural-language questions and generate conversational responses. They formed the AI foundation for the chatbot's career-guidance interactions.

### Prompt Engineering

Prompt engineering was important for defining the chatbot's persona, purpose, and response style. Instructions could guide the AI to communicate like a helpful student-focused assistant and keep its answers relevant to career-related queries.

### Conversational AI

Conversational AI was the central concept behind Master Ji Bot. It allowed the project to be designed around interactive question-and-answer communication rather than static information.

## 7. Development Approach

The development process began with understanding the problem and identifying the needs of college students. The project requirements were then considered in relation to the chatbot's intended persona and career-guidance functionality.

Working with my teammate, I explored how LLMs and prompt engineering could support the planned conversational experience. Antigravity IDE was used as the development environment during the project.

The work also involved considering different types of student queries and the importance of generating relevant, clear, and understandable responses.
