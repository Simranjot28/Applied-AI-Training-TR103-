# Day 18 — Master Ji Bot: Testing and Refinement

## 1. Objective

The objective of this session was to understand the importance of testing and refining Master Ji Bot, an AI-powered career-guidance chatbot developed as a group project. After working on the chatbot's purpose, persona, and functionality, the focus shifted towards evaluating its expected responses and identifying areas where its behaviour could be improved.

## 2. Topics Covered

### Importance of Testing AI Chatbots

Testing helps determine whether a chatbot responds appropriately to different user queries. Unlike a traditional program that may follow fixed rules, an AI chatbot can generate different responses depending on the input and context.

For Master Ji Bot, testing needed to consider whether the generated guidance was relevant to college students and aligned with the chatbot's career-guidance purpose.

### Relevance and Clarity of Responses

A useful career-guidance chatbot should understand the intent of a student's question and provide information that addresses it.

Important response-quality factors included:

- **Relevance:** The answer should address the question asked.
- **Clarity:** The response should be understandable to college students.
- **Usefulness:** The answer should offer meaningful guidance or practical next steps.
- **Consistency:** The chatbot should maintain its intended student-guide persona.
- **Limitations:** The chatbot should not present uncertain career-related information as guaranteed facts.

### Refinement of Chatbot Behaviour

Refinement involves reviewing the chatbot's behaviour and improving its instructions or interaction flow where necessary. Prompt engineering can help address issues such as overly general answers, unclear explanations, or responses that do not match the user's question.

Testing also helps identify situations where a chatbot may misunderstand an input or generate an answer that sounds convincing but is not sufficiently reliable.

## 3. Research and Practical Work

During this session, I continued exploring the testing and refinement process for Master Ji Bot with my teammate. We considered examples of career-related questions that college students might ask and the qualities expected in useful chatbot responses.

I examined the importance of checking whether an answer was connected to the user's question, whether the explanation was easy to understand, and whether the guidance matched the chatbot's intended purpose.

I also explored how prompt instructions and conversation flow could influence response quality. This helped me understand that developing an AI chatbot involves more than generating responses; its behaviour must also be reviewed against the needs of its intended users.

## 4. Tools and Technologies

- **Antigravity IDE:** Development environment for the project.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Studied for AI-generated conversational responses.
- **Prompt engineering:** Explored as a way to guide and refine chatbot behaviour.
- **Conversational AI:** Used as the underlying concept for the career-guidance assistant.
- **Test queries and response review:** Considered for evaluating relevance and clarity.

## 5. Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, I understood why testing is an important part of developing AI-powered applications. I learned that chatbot responses should be evaluated for relevance, clarity, usefulness, and consistency rather than judged only by whether the system generates an answer.

I also gained a better understanding of how reviewing test queries can help identify potential weaknesses and guide further refinement. These concepts provided a foundation for the project's final review and preparation.
