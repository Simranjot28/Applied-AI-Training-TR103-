# Day 21 — InterviewAI: Core Features and Interview Workflow

## 1. Objective

The objective of this session was to identify and organise the core features of InterviewAI and understand how they could work together to provide a complete interview preparation experience. After understanding the project's purpose and development environment, the focus shifted towards planning the user journey, selecting important modules, and connecting AI functionality with the interview preparation process.

## 2. Topics Covered

### Core Features of InterviewAI

InterviewAI was planned as a platform that could support students at different stages of interview preparation. Instead of focusing only on question generation, the project aimed to cover multiple aspects of preparation.

The major modules considered for the platform included:

- **User Authentication and Profile:** Allowing students to access the platform and maintain their profile information.
- **Target Role Selection:** Helping users select a job role or technical position for which they want to prepare.
- **Resume Analysis:** Exploring resume evaluation and ATS-related feedback.
- **Skill Gap Analysis:** Identifying skills that may need improvement based on the selected role and available profile information.
- **Learning Roadmap:** Providing direction for improving relevant skills.
- **Aptitude Preparation:** Supporting practice for aptitude-related questions.
- **AI Interview Practice:** Allowing users to practise technical and HR-based interview questions.
- **Dashboard:** Presenting relevant preparation information and progress.

### Interview Preparation Workflow

A workflow describes the sequence of steps a user follows while using an application. For InterviewAI, the planned user journey connected the different preparation modules.

The general workflow involved:

1. The student accesses the platform through authentication.
2. The student provides profile information and selects a target role.
3. The platform uses available resume information to support preparation.
4. Skill-related gaps and areas for improvement are considered.
5. The student can follow a preparation roadmap and practise aptitude questions.
6. The student participates in AI-powered technical or HR interview sessions.
7. The platform presents feedback and relevant preparation information.

This structure helped establish how the different modules could contribute to the overall interview preparation experience.

### AI-Based Interview Practice

One of the central features of InterviewAI was the AI interview practice module. LLMs could be used to generate questions based on the selected role, company context, and difficulty level.

The project also explored the idea of evaluating a user's answers and providing feedback. Useful feedback could include strengths, missing concepts, areas of weakness, and suggestions for improving an answer.

Prompt engineering was important in guiding the AI interviewer to generate relevant questions and provide structured feedback.

## 3. Research and Practical Work

During this session, I explored the main modules required for InterviewAI and considered how they could be organised into a connected user journey. I worked on understanding the relationship between the different preparation features, from selecting a target role to participating in an AI-powered interview.

I also explored how LLMs and prompt engineering could support question generation and answer evaluation. Particular attention was given to the interview practice workflow because it was one of the most important parts of the platform.

This planning helped establish a clearer structure for the project and provided direction for developing its individual features.

## 4. Tools and Technologies

- **Antigravity IDE:** Used as the development environment.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Explored for generating interview questions and evaluating responses.
- **Prompt engineering:** Used to guide AI interviewer behaviour and feedback structure.
- **Web application development:** Used for organising the platform's modules and user interface.
- **Conversational AI:** Applied to interactive interview practice.
