# Day 16 — Master Ji Bot: Student Persona and Career-Guidance Flow

## 1. Objective

The objective of this session was to develop the concept of Master Ji Bot further by defining its student-focused persona and planning how it would guide users through career-related conversations. After identifying the project's purpose and initial requirements, the focus shifted towards making the chatbot's responses relevant, understandable, and useful for college students.

## 2. Topics Covered

### Student-Focused Chatbot Persona

A chatbot persona defines the role, behaviour, tone, and communication style that an AI assistant should follow. For Master Ji Bot, the intended persona was that of a helpful guide who could communicate with college students in a friendly and understandable manner.

The chatbot needed to provide guidance without making conversations unnecessarily complicated. Its responses were expected to be suitable for students who might be exploring career options or learning about a technical field for the first time.

### Career-Guidance Conversation Flow

A conversation flow describes how a chatbot should respond to a user's question and continue the interaction. For Master Ji Bot, the planned flow centred on understanding the student's query and providing relevant career guidance.

The conversation could involve questions about:

- Different career paths available after studying a particular subject.
- Technical and professional skills required for a chosen field.
- Learning resources and ways to begin developing those skills.
- Preparation for internships, projects, and future job opportunities.

The responses needed to remain connected to the student's question rather than providing general information unrelated to the conversation.

### Role of Prompt Engineering

Prompt engineering was relevant to defining the chatbot's instructions and response behaviour. A well-defined prompt can specify the assistant's role, intended audience, purpose, and preferred response style.

For Master Ji Bot, the student-guide persona could be described through instructions asking the AI to explain career concepts clearly, provide practical suggestions, and keep its answers relevant to the user's needs.

## 3. Research and Practical Work

During this session, I worked with my teammate to explore how Master Ji Bot could interact with college students and respond to career-related queries. We considered the type of guidance students might need and how the chatbot's persona could make the interaction more approachable.

I also explored the relationship between prompt engineering and chatbot behaviour. This helped me understand how instructions can influence an AI assistant's tone, role, and response structure.

The planning focused on establishing a logical conversation flow and connecting the chatbot's intended behaviour with the project's original requirements. These activities helped prepare the project for further development and testing.

## 4. Tools and Technologies

- **Antigravity IDE:** Development environment used for the project.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Studied for generating conversational responses.
- **Prompt engineering:** Used as a concept for defining the chatbot's persona and instructions.
- **Conversational AI:** Applied to the planned student-guidance interaction.

## 5. Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, I understood the importance of defining a clear persona and conversation flow when developing an AI chatbot. I learned how prompt engineering can help guide an AI assistant's behaviour and make its responses more suitable for a specific audience.

I also gained a better understanding of how a student-focused chatbot can organise career-related information into useful, understandable guidance. This work established a clearer direction for the next stage of the Master Ji Bot project.
