# Day 15 — Master Ji Bot: Project Introduction and Requirements

## 1. Objective

The objective of this session was to begin working on Master Ji Bot, a group project undertaken with one other team member during the Applied AI training. The project focused on developing an AI-powered chatbot to guide college students regarding career opportunities, relevant skills, and professional development. The main goal was to understand the problem, identify the intended users, and establish the initial requirements before moving towards implementation.

## 2. Topics Covered

### Project Introduction

Master Ji Bot was planned as a student-oriented AI chatbot that could interact with college students through natural-language conversations. Its purpose was to make career guidance more accessible and help students explore different professional paths.

The idea behind the project was to create a conversational assistant that students could approach with questions about their careers and future goals. The project also provided an opportunity to apply concepts from the earlier training sessions to a practical AI application.

### Problem Identification and Target Users

College students often have questions about career choices, required technical skills, available opportunities, and the steps they should take to prepare for their future. Students who are still exploring different fields may find it difficult to decide where to begin.

Master Ji Bot was designed around these needs, with college students identified as its primary target users. Understanding the target audience helped establish the type of guidance the chatbot should provide and the communication style it should follow.

### Initial Project Requirements

The initial planning focused on identifying the main purpose and expected behaviour of the chatbot. The requirements considered for the project included:

- Providing a conversational interface for college students.
- Answering career-related questions in simple and understandable language.
- Helping students explore career paths and relevant skills.
- Maintaining relevance to the user's question.
- Providing guidance suited to students who may be uncertain about their future career direction.

These requirements provided a starting point for deciding how the chatbot should interact with users.

### Role of AI in Master Ji Bot

Large Language Models (LLMs) were relevant to the project because they can interpret natural-language questions and generate conversational responses. Prompt engineering was another important concept because instructions can help define the chatbot's persona, purpose, and response style.

The student-focused persona was intended to make interactions approachable and understandable. At the same time, career guidance needed to be treated as assistance that supports students' decisions rather than as a guarantee of a particular outcome.

## 3. Research and Practical Work

During this session, I worked with my teammate to understand the project idea and discuss the needs of the intended users. We explored how an AI-powered chatbot could help college students obtain career-related guidance through natural-language conversations.

I considered the connection between the project's requirements and the AI concepts studied during the earlier training sessions, particularly LLMs and prompt engineering. I also explored the types of questions students might ask and the importance of providing clear, relevant, and easy-to-understand responses.

The initial work focused on understanding the problem, identifying the chatbot's purpose, and establishing a direction for the project. This planning provided a foundation for the development activities that would follow.
