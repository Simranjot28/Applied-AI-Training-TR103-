# Day 24 — InterviewAI: Final Review, Project Outcomes, and Training Learnings

## 1. Objective

The objective of this session was to review the overall development of InterviewAI and reflect on the knowledge and practical experience gained throughout the Applied AI training. The focus was on revisiting the project's main features, understanding how different AI concepts were applied, and evaluating the learning outcomes of developing an AI-powered interview preparation platform.

## 2. Topics Covered

### Overall Project Review

InterviewAI was developed with the objective of making interview preparation more interactive and accessible for students. The platform explored how AI could support different stages of preparation, from selecting a target role and understanding skill requirements to practising interviews and receiving feedback.

The major areas covered in the project included:

- User authentication and student profile management.
- Target role selection and resume-related functionality.
- Skill gap analysis and preparation roadmap.
- Aptitude practice.
- AI-powered technical and HR interview preparation.
- Answer evaluation and structured feedback.
- Dashboard for presenting relevant preparation information.

Reviewing these modules helped establish how different application features could work together to support a student's interview preparation journey.

### Application of Applied AI Concepts

The project provided an opportunity to connect the concepts studied during the training with practical development work.

Some of the important concepts applied or explored included:

**Large Language Models (LLMs):** Used for understanding natural-language input and generating AI-based responses.

**Prompt Engineering:** Explored for defining the AI interviewer's role, generating relevant questions, and structuring feedback.

**API Integration:** Used to understand how the application communicates with an external AI model through API requests and responses.

**Conversational AI:** Applied to create an interactive interview practice experience.

**Testing and Debugging:** Used to review application functionality, identify issues, and consider improvements in AI-generated responses.

### Importance of AI in Interview Preparation

AI can make interview preparation more interactive by allowing students to practise questions and receive feedback without depending entirely on another person.

However, an AI-based interview system also needs to consider response relevance, evaluation quality, and the limitations of generated feedback. This is important because the purpose of the platform is to support learning and improvement rather than simply generate answers.

## 3. Research and Practical Work

During this final session, I reviewed the overall direction and functionality of InterviewAI. I reflected on the development activities carried out during the project and the connection between the application's modules and the concepts studied during training.

Working with Antigravity IDE provided practical exposure to developing an AI-integrated application. The use of Gemini 2.5 Flash helped me understand how an external AI model could support question generation and response-related functionality.

I also reviewed the importance of connecting frontend functionality, backend processing, and AI integration to create a complete application. The project helped me understand the practical challenges involved in developing an AI-based system, particularly when dealing with generated responses and their evaluation.

## 4. Tools and Technologies

- **Antigravity IDE:** Used as the development environment.
- **React and Vite:** Used for frontend development.
- **Python and FastAPI:** Used for backend functionality.
- **Gemini 2.5 Flash:** AI model used for AI-powered features.
- **Prompt engineering:** Used to guide AI behaviour and response generation.
- **API integration:** Used to connect the application with AI functionality.
- **Testing and debugging:** Explored for reviewing application behaviour and identifying issues.

## 5. Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, I had gained practical exposure to the process of developing an AI-powered application, from understanding the problem and planning features to exploring AI integration, testing, and reviewing the final functionality.

The Applied AI training strengthened my understanding of LLMs, prompt engineering, APIs, embeddings, RAG, Agentic AI, and conversational AI. It also helped me understand how these concepts can be connected to solve practical problems through software applications.

Working on Master Ji Bot as a group project and InterviewAI as an individual project gave me experience with two different applications of AI. Overall, the training helped me move beyond theoretical understanding and gain a clearer perspective on how AI concepts can be explored and applied in real-world projects.
