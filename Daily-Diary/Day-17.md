# Day 17 — Master Ji Bot: Development and Implementation

## 1. Objective

The objective of this session was to move forward with the development of Master Ji Bot, a group project designed to provide career guidance to college students. Building on the requirements and conversation flow discussed earlier, the focus was on exploring how the planned chatbot could be developed using an AI-based development environment and conversational AI concepts.

## 2. Topics Covered

### Translating Requirements into a Chatbot

After identifying the project's purpose and target users, the next step was to connect the requirements with the chatbot's expected behaviour. This involved considering how students would ask questions and how the system should provide useful responses.

The main areas of focus included:

- Accepting career-related questions in natural language.
- Generating responses that are clear and relevant to students.
- Maintaining the chatbot's student-friendly persona.
- Providing guidance related to career paths, skills, and learning opportunities.

### AI-Powered Response Generation

Large Language Models (LLMs) can interpret natural-language input and generate responses based on the context and instructions provided to them. This made them relevant to the planned functionality of Master Ji Bot.

I explored how the chatbot's instructions and persona could guide response generation. I also considered the importance of giving the AI a clear purpose so that its answers would remain aligned with the career-guidance objective.

### User Interaction and Response Quality

A useful chatbot should do more than generate text. Its responses should address the user's actual question and be easy to understand.

For a student-focused assistant, response quality involves relevance, clarity, appropriate detail, and practical usefulness. If a student asks about a career path, for example, the response should ideally explain the field and the skills or preparation associated with it.

## 3. Research and Practical Work

During this session, I continued working with my teammate on the Master Ji Bot project. We explored how the planned requirements and student-focused persona could be translated into the chatbot's functionality.

Using Antigravity IDE as the development environment, I worked on understanding the project-development process and the relationship between the chatbot's intended behaviour and its AI-based responses.

I also considered how different career-related questions could be used to examine whether the chatbot's responses were relevant and understandable. This helped connect the concepts studied during training, particularly LLMs and prompt engineering, with the development of a practical AI application.

## 4. Tools and Technologies

- **Antigravity IDE:** Environment used for project development.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Studied for natural-language understanding and response generation.
- **Prompt engineering:** Used to guide the chatbot's role and response style.
- **Conversational AI:** Applied to the student-guidance use case.
- **Team collaboration:** Used to coordinate project work and discuss functionality.

## 5. Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, I gained a better understanding of how AI concepts can be translated into the functionality of a chatbot. I learned the importance of connecting project requirements, prompt instructions, and response generation when developing a conversational AI application.

This session also helped me understand that successful chatbot development requires attention to both functionality and user experience. The next stage would involve checking the chatbot's responses, identifying possible issues, and refining its behaviour where needed.
