# Day 20 — InterviewAI: Project Introduction and Setup

## 1. Objective

The objective of this session was to begin working on InterviewAI, an individual project focused on AI-powered interview preparation. The aim was to explore how artificial intelligence could help students prepare for technical and behavioural interviews, practise answering questions, and understand areas where they could improve.

This session focused on understanding the problem, identifying the project's main objectives, and becoming familiar with the development environment before proceeding with the implementation.

## 2. Topics Covered

### Introduction to InterviewAI

InterviewAI was planned as an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students practise for job interviews. Traditional preparation methods often involve reading interview questions, studying answers, or practising with another person. An AI-based platform can provide a more interactive way to practise and review responses.

The project explored how AI could support interview preparation by generating relevant questions, allowing users to practise their answers, and providing feedback to help them identify areas for improvement.

### Problem Identification and Project Objectives

Students preparing for interviews may struggle with selecting appropriate questions, evaluating their own answers, and identifying concepts that need further preparation.

The main objectives of InterviewAI included:

- Supporting interview practice through AI-generated questions.
- Helping users prepare for technical and behavioural interviews.
- Making practice more interactive than simply reading question-and-answer material.
- Providing feedback that can help users understand the strengths and weaknesses of their responses.

### Development Environment

Antigravity IDE was used as the development environment for the project. An AI-powered development environment can assist with understanding project structure, exploring implementation approaches, and developing application features.

Before implementing the project, it was important to understand the overall requirements and consider how the user interface, application logic, and AI functionality would work together.

### Role of AI in Interview Preparation

LLMs can generate interview questions, interpret natural-language answers, and produce feedback based on the information provided. Prompt engineering can help guide the AI to behave like an interviewer and structure its responses in a useful format.

However, AI-generated feedback needs to be relevant to the question and the user's answer. This is particularly important in interview preparation, where misleading or overly general feedback may not help the learner improve.
