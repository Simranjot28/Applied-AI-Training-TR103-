# Day 19 — Master Ji Bot: Final Review and Project Learnings

## 1. Objective

The objective of this session was to review the work completed on Master Ji Bot, a group project developed to provide AI-powered career guidance to college students. The focus was on revisiting the project's purpose, considering the chatbot's intended functionality, and reflecting on how concepts learned during the Applied AI training could be applied to a practical project.

## 2. Topics Covered

### Review of Project Requirements

The project was designed around the career-guidance needs of college students. The review focused on the original objective of helping students explore career paths, understand relevant skills, and obtain guidance through natural-language conversations.

Revisiting these requirements helped connect the project's intended functionality with the needs of its target users.

### Application of Applied AI Concepts

Master Ji Bot provided an opportunity to explore several concepts studied during the training:

- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Their role in understanding questions and generating conversational responses.
- **Prompt engineering:** Defining the chatbot's persona, purpose, and response style through instructions.
- **Conversational AI:** Designing interactions around questions and answers in natural language.
- **Testing and refinement:** Reviewing response relevance and identifying possible improvements.

These concepts demonstrated how AI knowledge can be used to plan and develop an application around a specific problem.

### Importance of Teamwork and Iterative Development

Working on Master Ji Bot with a teammate helped me understand the importance of discussing requirements, sharing ideas, and maintaining a common understanding of the project's goals.

The project also highlighted the iterative nature of software development. Requirements, chatbot behaviour, and response quality may need to be reviewed and refined as development progresses.

## 3. Research and Practical Work

During this session, I reviewed the project's overall direction and reflected on the work carried out during the Master Ji Bot phase. I considered how the chatbot's student-focused persona and career-guidance purpose were connected to the AI concepts studied earlier.

I also consolidated my understanding of the development and testing considerations involved in a conversational AI project. The review helped me recognise the importance of defining a clear problem, understanding the target users, and evaluating the usefulness of AI-generated responses.

This phase gave me practical exposure to collaborative AI project work and prepared me to apply the concepts from the training to my next project, InterviewAI.

## 4. Tools and Technologies

- **Antigravity IDE:** Development environment used during the project.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Studied for conversational response generation.
- **Prompt engineering:** Explored for defining chatbot behaviour.
- **Conversational AI:** Used as the central concept behind the project.
- **Team collaboration:** Supported project planning, discussion, and review.

## 5. Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, I had developed a clearer understanding of how AI concepts can be combined to build a student-focused conversational assistant. I learned the importance of connecting project requirements with chatbot behaviour and considering response quality throughout development.

The Master Ji Bot phase also strengthened my understanding of collaborative project work, iterative improvement, and the practical use of LLMs and prompt engineering. With this phase complete, I was ready to move on to my individual InterviewAI project and explore a different application of AI.
