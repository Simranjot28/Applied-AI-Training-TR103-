# Day 2 — Introduction to Large Language Models (LLMs)

## 1. Objective

The objective of this session was to understand Large Language Models (LLMs), their fundamental working principles, capabilities, and limitations. The session focused on how these models process text inputs, identify patterns in language, and generate meaningful responses. Another objective was to explore different applications of LLMs and understand their importance in developing modern AI-powered applications.

## 2. Topics Covered

### Understanding Large Language Models

A Large Language Model (LLM) is an artificial intelligence model designed to process and generate human language. It learns patterns, relationships, and structures from large amounts of training data. Based on these learned patterns, an LLM can generate text, answer questions, summarize information, explain concepts, and assist with programming tasks.

We discussed how LLMs predict the next token based on the context available to them. A token can represent a complete word, part of a word, or another unit of text. By generating tokens sequentially, the model produces responses that are generally relevant to the user's input.

### Capabilities and Applications of LLMs

LLMs are widely used in conversational assistants, educational tools, content generation, document analysis, coding assistance, and customer support systems. They can help users understand complex topics, generate ideas, and perform language-related tasks more efficiently.

Some important capabilities discussed were:

- **Text generation:** Producing explanations, articles, descriptions, and other written content.
- **Question answering:** Responding to user queries based on learned patterns and available context.
- **Summarization:** Converting lengthy information into shorter, meaningful summaries.
- **Code assistance:** Explaining code, suggesting solutions, and helping identify programming errors.
- **Conversational interaction:** Maintaining context across multiple messages within the available context window.

### Limitations of LLMs

Although LLMs can perform many useful tasks, their responses are not always accurate. They may generate incorrect information confidently, misunderstand ambiguous questions, or provide answers that lack sufficient context. This behavior is commonly associated with AI hallucinations.

We also discussed the importance of the context window, which determines how much information a model can process within a particular interaction. When relevant information is missing or exceeds the model's available context, the quality of the response may be affected.

Another important concept was the difference between open and closed models. Open-weight models may allow developers to download, customize, and deploy model weights under their respective licenses, whereas closed models are generally accessed through provider-controlled interfaces or APIs.
